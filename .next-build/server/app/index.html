01The concept

A studio, not a catalogue.

INKVERSE is a digital tattoo studio. Three things have to be true for that to mean anything: the body has to be real, the ink has to sit on it, and the interface has to stay out of the way.

Our approach
01

Real 3D body

Two studio characters built on a licensed anatomical mesh. Orbit 360°, zoom, and switch between front, back and profile without losing your composition.

02

Ink on the surface

Designs are placed in UV space and composited in the skin shader, so every line follows the curvature of the body — not a plane floating in front of it.

03

Editorial, not dashboard

A dark studio interface with restrained controls. Seventeen mapped body zones, eight independent layers, and an export that is honest about what it is.

02Selected works

The vault, opened.

A working selection from the studio library. Every piece is original vector artwork, drawn for a specific zone of the body.

03The 3D studio

Turn the body.
Move the ink.

Select a zone by clicking the character or the zone list. Choose a design, then size, rotate and position it with immediate feedback. The composition persists while you orbit — undo, compare and export when it is right.

  • 17 anatomical zones on both characters
  • Eight independent tattoo layers, 0.02–1.8 m
  • Undo / redo, compare view, PNG capture
  • Shareable links that carry the whole composition
