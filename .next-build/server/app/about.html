01Anatomy

A body you can inspect.

Both characters start from the MakeHuman CC0 base mesh — a 13,380-vertex anatomical body with a clean, non-overlapping UV layout. We morph it into a studio build, normalise it to 1.86 m, and bake skin detail from the topology itself.

Geometry

14,517 vertices · 26,756 triangles per character. Exported to GLB with tangents, normals and a single UV set.

Skin

Procedural PBR albedo baked at 2048²: tonal variation, flush, brows, lash line, stubble, scalp and baked AO from real vertex occlusion.

Zones

Nearest-bone segmentation of the mesh into 17 regions, baked as a per-texel region ID texture used for hit-testing, masking and highlighting.

03Under the hood

The ink engine.

A short technical account of how a design gets onto skin — and stays there while you orbit.

  1. 01

    UV islands

    The mesh is segmented into connected UV islands. Each of the 17 body regions owns one or more islands with a measured metres-per-UV-unit scale.

  2. 02

    Physical placement

    A design is a square plate sized in metres. Its UV half-extents are derived from the region’s measured stretch, so the piece is physically square on skin.

  3. 03

    Ink atlas

    Up to eight designs are rasterised into 16 tiles of a 2048² density atlas at runtime. Density comes from artwork luminance, so paper-coloured negative space stays skin.

  4. 04

    Skin shader

    The skin material samples the atlas in each pixel’s tattoo-local space, gated by the region ID texture, then multiplies the ink into the albedo beneath the specular layer.

Performance budget

  • Model: 619 KB per character (GLB, Draco-ready pipeline)
  • Skin: 110–122 KB albedo, 503 KB shared normal map
  • Renderer: DPR capped at 1.9, shadows 1024², contact shadows 512²
  • 3D code is client-only and dynamically imported; the gallery ships no WebGL
  • prefers-reduced-motion disables the hero orbit and page motion
04Known limitations

What this is not.

Every product has a boundary. These are ours, stated up front so nobody discovers them the hard way.

  • The studio characters are an athletic male and an athletic female build, both 1.86 m. There is no body-type or skin-tone selector yet.
  • Placement is a square plate inside a mapped UV island. It follows curvature perfectly but cannot wrap a design around the full circumference of an arm or leg.
  • Zones on the hands, feet, face and fingers are deliberately excluded — the surface is too small for a faithful square placement.
  • The ink preview does not simulate healing, ageing, blowout, stretch or the artist’s hand. It is a placement and scale tool, not a medical or artistic guarantee.
  • Saved compositions and favourites are stored in this browser only. There is no account system, so nothing syncs between devices.
  • Share links are self-contained and can be long. Links beyond 1600 characters are rejected rather than silently truncated.
